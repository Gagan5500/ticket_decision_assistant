# Damaged and Defective Goods Policy

## 1. Reporting Window for Damaged, Broken, Shattered, or Defective Items
- Customers must report damaged, broken, shattered, cracked, or crushed items within 48 hours of confirmed delivery.
- Reports made after 48 hours are not eligible for standard transit damage claims and require managerial escalation.

## 2. Evidence & Documentation Requirements
- For orders with total value **up to ₹2,000**: A replacement or refund may be authorized based on the customer's detailed incident description of the broken or damaged product.
- For orders with total value **exceeding ₹2,000**: Clear photographic evidence showing the damaged item, barcode/serial number, and exterior packaging is **strictly mandatory**. If photos have not been provided yet, the customer support agent must request photographs (`REQUEST_PHOTOS`).

## 3. Resolution Actions for Damaged or Broken Merchandise
- **REQUEST_PHOTOS**: If the claim is for an order exceeding ₹2,000 and the customer has not attached or provided photos.
- **APPROVE_REPLACEMENT**: If valid photos are provided or order is under ₹2,000, and the customer prefers a replacement item.
- **APPROVE_REFUND**: If valid photos are verified and the item is out of stock or customer requests a refund.
- **REJECT_CLAIM**: If damage is identified as caused by improper use, alteration, or intentional mishandling after delivery.
- **NEEDS_MORE_INFORMATION**: If critical details such as the order ID, exact item condition, or delivery date are missing.
